History

Learn

See the liquid. Hear the science. Liquid Analyzer explores how liquids respond to calibrated sound waves.

📷

1. Camera + Sound

Your camera frames the container for visual context. The measurement itself comes from calibrated sound waves emitted by your speaker and recorded by your microphone—the camera image never determines composition.

🧪

2. Experimental Reference Matching

Liquid Analyzer extracts resonance and decay features from the recorded echo and estimates whether they resemble a characterized reference liquid profile.

📊

3. Reference Composition

When a scan matches a reference profile, the Composition card shows that profile's known constituents. Outputs are experimental estimates for research and education—never a purity, potability, or safety test.

♫

4. Chemical Tune

Chemical Tune converts mapped reference constituents and measured acoustic resonances into musical intervals and timbres through a defined audio mapping.
