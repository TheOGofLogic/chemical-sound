## 3. Verdict on `Sweep 1` vs. `Sweep 2` Conditioning (`TEST-01` + Raw Pre-Roll Forensics)

> [!TIP]
> Inspecting `frames 0..3000` (`0–62.5 ms` early pre-roll) versus `frames 9000..directStart` (`just before ESS`) proved that **`Sweep 2`'s Build 12 SNR depression was caused by decaying `Sweep 1` acoustic/audio-session bleed at the front of `Sweep 2`'s `650 ms`-spaced pre-roll buffer**:
> - **`C: Salt-Water` (`SCAN_0073_rep02.wav`):** Early pre-roll `[0–62.5 ms]` started at **`-50.48 dBFS`** (`+32.26 dB` above `Sweeps 3–20`'s `-82.74 dBFS` median) and decayed by `15.15 dB` to `-65.63 dBFS` before excitation began—explaining Build 12's exact `19.63 dB` SNR drop.
> - **`B: Water` (`SCAN_0033_rep02.wav`) & `F: Cooking Oil` (`SCAN_0153_rep02.wav`):** Early pre-roll started **`+12.11 dB`** and **`+10.42 dB`** elevated (`-70.88` and `-69.99 dBFS`), decaying cleanly before excitation.
> - **Reactive-Mode Settling (`D: Peroxide`, `E: Vinegar`):** Even without pre-roll bleed (`+1.7` to `+2.2 dB`), tracked `~2.76 kHz` modes shifted by `-8.9` to `-11.0 Hz` between `Sweep 2` and `Sweeps 3–20`.

**R2-B Conditioning Specification:**
1. Measure ambient pre-roll noise strictly from the **late pre-roll segment (`[directStart - 95 ms .. directStart - 10 ms]`)** (and deconvolved noise strictly before the Farina harmonic pre-impulse zone `[9600 .. n0 - 9800]`), never `[0..62 ms]`.
2. Increase inter-sweep warm-engine quiet settle from `650 ms` to **`>= 900 ms`** and support configurable `K_cond ∈ {1, 2}` (defaulting to **`K_cond = 2` for `20×` calibration/audit bursts** so `Sweeps 3–20` are 100% free of both pre-roll bleed and `Sweep 2` meniscus/bubble settling).

## 5. Mandatory Estimator Upgrades Validated for `R2-B` (`MODE_A` Implementation)

1. **Replace Raw STFT Decay Regression with Chu-Compensated Narrowband Schroeder EDC:** On the 140 real airborne WAVs, multipath interference beats reduced raw STFT log-envelope median $R^2$ to `0.05–0.67` (**a premature `R² ≥ 0.85` gate would have rejected `459 / 461` = `99.57%` of real observations**). Narrowband Schroeder backward integration (`EDC`) over `[2.15..15.5 ms]` raised median $R^2$ to **`0.64–0.995`** and cleanly resolved `363–6,251 dB/s` decays without Build 12's `145.0 dB/s` clamp.
2. **Use `E1` (3-Point Log-Parabolic) + Continuity-Preserving `ModalAssociationTracker`:** Achieved **`0.069%–0.449%` mean within-burst CV** across all 7 real cohorts (collapsing `Salt`, `Peroxide`, and `Vinegar` single-peak CVs from `9.7%–14.8%` down to `< 0.45%`) and `94.44%` mode-crossing continuity (`PC-07`).
3. **Pair Passive Reflected $S_{\text{HF}}$ with Active Pre-Roll Fizz Elevation:** Bare empty vessels ring strongly at `5.5–8.5 kHz`, whereas only active gas-evolving/effervescent samples simultaneously elevate **quiet pre-roll high-frequency acoustic noise** (`Peroxide: -115.3 dB` vs. `Empty: -125.8 dB`, `Water: -126.0 dB`). Combining both prevents empty containers from triggering `BUBBLE_SCATTER_DETECTED`.
4. **Normalize $S_{\text{att}}$ by $|G_{\text{ESS}}(k)|^2$ & Reference $Q_i$ Resolution to $\Delta f_{\text{win},-3\text{dB}}$ (`108 Hz`), Never Zero-Padded $\Delta f_{\text{bin}}$ (`5.86 Hz`).**
