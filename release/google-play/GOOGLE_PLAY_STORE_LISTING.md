## 4. Full Description
Liquid Analyzer transforms your Android phone into an everyday fluid inspection and educational liquid laboratory. Using multi-modal sensing—combining camera color, turbidity, and meniscus analysis with acoustic container resonance and micro-bubble detection—Liquid Analyzer helps you screen, compare, and explore everyday household fluids in seconds.

KEY FEATURES:

• MULTI-MODAL LIQUID IDENTIFICATION
Screen everyday fluids including cooking oil, milk, coffee, cola, citrus juice, red wine, and sparkling water using on-device optical colorimetry, turbidity, and 48 kHz acoustic resonance fusion.

• GUIDED 3-STEP CUP COMPARATOR
Compare clear household liquids in the same stationary cup using a guided Empty Cup → Water Blank → Sample differential workflow.

• INTERACTIVE COMPOSITION & CHEMICAL TUNES
Explore typical chemical formulas, nominal pH ranges, literature-based constituent breakdowns, and synthesized Chemical Tunes for matched liquids right on your screen.

• SMART SAFETY & CONTAINER AUDIT
Intelligent container boundary and coupling detection checks for real liquid presence, preventing false readings on dry empty glassware, mugs, or flat solid surfaces.

• AUDIO READOUT & ACCESSIBILITY
Instant spoken voice readouts (`TextToSpeech`) and clear sensor attribution cards show how optical, acoustic, and effervescence signals contributed to each result.

• 100% PRIVATE & OFFLINE
All visual and acoustic processing runs entirely on your device. No camera images, audio recordings, or sensor streams are ever uploaded to cloud servers.

Note: Liquid Analyzer is designed for educational, informational, and general consumer household utility. Reference compositions reflect standard literature profiles for matched liquid classes and are not intended for clinical diagnostics, toxic chemical analysis, drinkability testing, or medical safety verification.
